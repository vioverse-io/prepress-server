      A component on the work order (the Letter, the Envelope, the Buckslip) is called a piece in QC Review. They are the same thing.

      Choosing your name

      When the app opens:

      1. Type part of your name and click it.
      2. Press Next.
      3. Check your name under You are choosing. Press Continue, or Back to pick again.
      • Choosing your name never asks for a password.
      • Always pick your own name. Your work is saved under it, every QC Review check included.
      • Your name shows at the top right. This browser keeps it until you press Change user in your profile, or until 12 hours pass without using the app.
      • Not on the list? Contact an administrator or the IT department.

      The job list

      Every active job, 10 to a page. Click a row to open its work order (the QC cell opens QC Review instead). Click a column's header to sort by it (not Depts).

      Filters

      • My Jobs: the jobs you created. All Jobs: every active job.
      • Search: job number, client, project, CSR, and both departments' assignees.
      • Show Corrections: jobs whose QC chip reads CORRECTIONS. The counts on Show cover all active jobs, whatever else is filtered.
      • Dept: All, Prepress or Tech Services. See More detail for what counts.
      • CSR, Assignee, Client: check names, then press Apply. Your picks show as chips under the filters.

      Columns

      • Signoff Due: the earlier of the two departments' dates. A date with no time counts as 5:00 PM.
      • OVERDUE (red): the date has passed. DUE SOON (blue): it is due later today or tomorrow.
      • Complete and Cancelled jobs show the date in grey.
      • Status: click it on any row to change it. Complete asks Archive this job? Yes archives it, No keeps it on the list.
      • If somebody has that job open, the status change is put back and the app names them.
      • Depts: a red dot for Prepress, a navy dot for Tech Services.
      • Created: when the job was entered in the app, not the customer's in-hands date.
      • QC: where the job's QC Review stands. A click in that cell opens the QC Review window, never the work order. See the QC Review tab.

      Several jobs at once

      • Check the boxes at the left of the rows, or the header box for the whole page.
      • Press Archive or Delete on the bar that appears. Delete asks for the admin password.
      • A job somebody has open is skipped and named.

      Refresh loads everyone's latest changes. The page buttons and the per page choice are under the table.

      More detail

      A job counts for a department, and lights its dot in Depts, when any of these is true:

      • a component has instructions for it, in the box or in the Revisions
      • one of its rows is switched on
      • someone is assigned to it
      • it has QC Review going there

      A signoff due date alone does not count.

      Making a work order

      1. Press New Work Order on the job list, or + in the top bar.
      2. Fill in Job Number, Client Name and CSR Name (required) and Project Name.
      3. Under Components, click each component the job has. With none picked, the job gets one called "Main".
      4. For each department, set Assigned to and the signoff due date and time.
      5. Press Create.
      • A signoff due is first set in this form. Duplicate Job uses the same form.
      • Once a department has a signoff due, you can change it in the header. A department left without one cannot get one later on the work order.

      Duplicating

      • Duplicate Job (the ... menu, on a work order) opens this form with everything copied and " (Copy)" after the job number. Change the number before you press Create.
      • Duplicate Job does not copy:
        • the instructions and the Revisions
        • the file paths and the variable print rows
        • Previous Job#
        • both assignees and both signoff dates
        • the job's QC Review record
      • Duplicate Component copies the component showing into the same job, leaving out the same rows.

      The work order screen

      The header

      • Click Customer, CSR, Component (the name of the component showing), Signoff due, Assigned to, Version or Status to change it.
      • Signoff due and Assigned to belong to the department tab showing. Signoff due shows only when that tab has one.
      • + beside Version gives the next version: A01, A02, A03.
      • Send to Prepress and QC Review: see the QC Review tab. Send to Prepress is for CSRs only. On the Tech Services tab it reads Send to Tech Services.
      • Lock instructions: see "Saving and leaving" below.

      The checklist

      • Prepress and Tech Services each have their own tab, rows and instructions box.
      • Each row has a switch. Most rows also have a box for details. Typing in the box switches the row on.
      • The small arrow on a box lists common answers.
      • A path pasted into a File Path box or Data file locations loses its quotes, and M:, N: or X: becomes the server's name.
      • A * after a row's name marks it required. Its box has a dashed outline while empty.
      • Greyed-out rows do not apply to the component.
      • Click a section's title to fold it or open it.

      Instructions and Generate

      • The box on the left holds the department's instructions for the component showing. It takes bold, lists, highlight and pictures.
      • Generate writes a summary of the switched-on rows into the box, between two grey marker lines.
      • Press it again after a change. It rewrites the summary in place. Words outside the grey lines stay; words between them are replaced.
      • If the specs do not add up, a QC Issues list appears above the box. Example: a press that cannot print duplex. Generate still writes the summary.

      Revisions

      • The Revisions sit under the instructions box, newest first. Each is a round of instructions filed by Lock instructions, with who filed it and when.
      • The numbered buttons under them jump to one revision.
      • A revision cannot be taken back.

      Components

      • You can add components until a job has 10. Each has its own rows, instructions and version.
      • Their tabs are above the checklist. Click a tab to show it. Double-click it to rename it.
      • + adds one: type a name or pick one from the list.
      • A component tab's × deletes that component, after a question. The question says so if its QC checklist was started.
      • The last component cannot be deleted.

      More detail

      • Reset (in the ... menu) switches off the rows on the department tab showing, for the component showing. Typed details and the other department's rows stay. Undo brings the rows back.
      • To widen the instructions box, drag the edge between it and the checklist.
      • Greyed out on a Letter: Indicia. On an envelope: Print marks, Number Up, Head info and Binding info.
      • On an envelope, Flat Size and Press offer envelope sizes and presses.
      • Under the header fields: who created and last changed the job, and when. "Header edited" follows a change to any header field except Status and Version. "Duplicated from" marks a copy. "Deleted" lists deleted components.

      One person at a time

      Only one person can change a job at a time. Anyone else gets it read only, with a READ-ONLY tag in the header and a line at the top:

      • Don Marcotte is editing this job, last saved 2 min ago. The page keeps up with their saves while you watch. If they have left it, press Take over. It asks first. Their page then turns read only and lists anything they had not saved.
      • Unlocked. Nobody is editing this job now. Press Start editing.
      • You have this job open in another window. Use that window, or press Edit here.
      • Checking who has this job... A moment while the app asks.
      • A job left 20 minutes without typing or clicking is let go, so others can have it. Press Start editing to carry on.
      • Your own two windows can both edit your job. Each picks up what you save in the other: Updated with the changes saved in your other window.
      • Printing and QC Review work on a read-only work order. QC Review never takes the job from anyone.

      Saving and leaving

      • The app saves by itself: a moment after you stop typing, and every couple of seconds while you type.
      • If the server cannot be reached, a banner says so. Your changes wait in this page until it is back. Do not close or reload the page while the banner shows, or they are lost.
      • Home (the house, top left) goes back to the job list. The instructions stay in the box for the next person.
      • Lock instructions (in the header) files the instructions into the Revisions, under your name, and empties the box for the next round. It does this for every component, on both department tabs, then closes the job.
      • After Lock instructions, a note on the job list names any required rows still empty, for 5 seconds.
      • Change user (in your profile) asks to close the job, then files its instructions the same way. It files them even if you then cancel the name picker.
      • Archive files them too, once you say yes.
      • Closing the tab or the browser, or F5, saves what is on the screen. If a change is too big to send as the tab closes, Chrome asks "Leave site?". Press Cancel, wait a moment, then close again.
      • F5, or Back within 30 minutes of leaving, opens again the job you were editing in that tab. If somebody else has it by then, it opens read only.

      Printing

      The Print menu in the top bar prints the department tab showing, with only that department's rows and instructions:

      • Print Current: the component showing.
      • Print All Components: each component as its own print, one after another. Saving PDFs, you get one Save box each.
      • Print All as One PDF: every component in one file, each starting on a new page.

      For the PDF that goes into Midnight, use the Print menu. Ctrl+P prints the screen, not the work order sheet.

      Before it prints

      • It gets the latest saved copy first. If that takes a moment, it says Getting the latest copy to print...
      • If the server does not answer: The latest copy could not be checked. Press Print anyway or Cancel.
      • If rows changed since the last Generate: Summary outdated for the component. Update & Print writes the summary again and prints. Print as is prints what is there.

      The printed sheet

      • A row switched off but with words prints with an empty box. File Path rows, Record Counts included, print only when switched on.
      • A file path prints under its label, one line each, in a typewriter font. In Chrome's PDF viewer a triple-click copies just that path.
      • Only the app's own yellow highlight prints.
      • The PDF is named after the job and component, like "48127 - Letter". Print All as One PDF uses every component's name.
      • Printing the same component again adds "(v2)", "(v3)" to the name, even after a cancelled print.
      • That count is kept on each PC, so two PCs can each make a "(v2)".

      Archived and deleted jobs

      • Archive (the box in the top bar, or the ... menu) asks Archive this job?
      • Yes files the instructions into the Revisions and moves the job to the Archived list, below the job list.
      • If QC Review is running on the job, the question first says where it stands. See the QC Review tab.
      • An archived job cannot be changed until it is restored.
      • Delete (the ... menu) deletes the job for good. It asks for the admin password, then Permanently delete this job?
      • If the job had QC Review, its QC record is kept. See "Deleted jobs" in the QC Review tab.

      The Archived list

      • The Search archive box: job number, client and project, in archived jobs only.
      • View, or click the job's name: the work order, read only, with QC Review in its header. Print works. Home brings you back.
      • Restore: back to the job list.
      • The red ×: deletes it for good, after the admin password and a question.

      Import and Export

      • Export (the ... menu): check the jobs you want and save them as a .json file.
      • Import: pick a .json file. Each job in it shows as New (added), Up to date (left alone), Newer or Older than the job here.
      • Newer starts checked, Older does not. A checked job is replaced by the file's copy, its components and Revisions included.
      • A job somebody has open is skipped and named.
      • If the file would leave QC Review checklists without their component, it says so first. See the QC Review tab.

      Your profile

      • Click the square with your initial, top right. Your profile opens.
      • Your profile holds your name, Change user, a photo, and the name you want to be greeted by.
      • The name beside the square does nothing.
      • The photo, greeting name, text size and dark mode belong to this PC, not to you. The next person on this PC sees them too.

      For the admin

      The admin password is asked before:

      • deleting a job (the ... menu, the bulk bar, or the Archived list's red ×)
      • any change in Manage CSRs & Assignees

      Manage CSRs & Assignees (the ... menu) holds the three lists people are picked from: CSRs, Prepress and Tech Services.

      • Add a name.
      • Turn off someone who leaves. Names are never deleted, so old jobs keep them and the filters still find them. Turn on brings a name back.
      • Each row has a list of users: pick the user that person chooses when opening the app, then press Save on that row. The list reads "Not linked" until then.
      • QC Review needs that link. A person not linked is read only there and is left out of the emails.
      • Link matching names links every name to the user with the same words, like David Marra and David.Marra. Names spelled differently (Sue Foster, Susan.Foster) are linked by hand.
      • Email on / Email off, the switch on each row: Off means QC Review never puts them in To, on any list. The person stays on the list and can still be assigned and work the checklist.
      • Use Email off for someone on a department's list who does not do that department's QC. A new name starts with email on, unless that name is off on another list.
      • Old names on jobs: names on jobs but not on the lists, usually old spellings. Move one onto the right name and every job using it changes at once.

      The list a name is on decides what that person may do in QC Review:

      • Prepress names: the Prepress tab.
      • Tech Services names: the Tech Services tab.
      • CSRs: the CSR checklist, Sign back in, and Send to Prepress / Send to Tech Services. A CSR may also review a department's checklist once it is sent for review.

      Limits, and where things are kept

      • 10,000 active jobs. Adding components stops at 10 a job. 15 undo steps.
      • Jobs are kept on the shop's SQL Server, shared by everyone on the network.
      • Kept on this PC only: dark mode, text size, your photo and greeting name, the print (v2) count, and the guide tab you read last.

      What QC Review is for

      Before proofs go to a client, someone checks each department's work against a checklist. Then the CSR checks the sign-off proofs.

      QC Review is where these checks are done and recorded: who checked what and when, and what was sent back and why.

      A job is never finished. The proof often comes back from the client, so a job can go round many times. Each time is a round.

      QC Review opens in its own window, over the job list or the work order. It never takes a work order from whoever is editing it.

      Start here

      If you are the CSR

      1. When the work order is ready and its PDF is in Midnight, press Send to Prepress on its Prepress tab. On the Tech Services tab it is Send to Tech Services. Type what they need to know and press Send. A job for both departments gets one press on each tab.
      2. When a department sends you its files, they send you an email with the paths. They are also under Outputs on the CSR tab of the QC Review window.
      3. Check the sign-off proofs with the CSR checklist on the CSR tab, then press Mark passed.
      4. When the client asks for changes, update the work order and attach the new PDF in Midnight first. Then press Sign back in on the CSR tab.

      If you did the work (the operator)

      1. Open QC Review in the work order header, or click the job's QC cell on the job list. Stay on your department's tab.
      2. Press Send for review. Add a note for the reviewer if you like, then press Send it. You are now the operator for this round, and someone else checks your work.
      3. If a piece comes back for fixes, fix it, then press Fixed, ready for re-review.
      4. When every block has passed, press Send to CSR. Paste where the files are and press Send it.

      If you are checking it (the reviewer)

      1. The email "Ready for Prepress review" (or Tech Services) is your call. Open it with the email's Open QC Review link or the job list's QC cell.
      2. Open each block. Check off each line you have checked. Mark N/A what does not apply. Flag what is wrong, with a note saying what needs fixing.
      3. A block with nothing wrong: Mark passed. A block with flags: Corrections needed, then Send it back.
      4. When it comes back fixed, check the flagged lines again and pass it.

      The QC Review window

      Open it from any of these:

      • the job's QC cell on the job list
      • QC Review in the work order header
      • the Open QC Review link in an email
      • a deleted job's QC record button

      Each tab is a checklist: Prepress, Tech Services and CSR. A checklist is in blocks:

      • Prepress: one block per piece, and a Whole Job block.
      • Tech Services and CSR: one block for the whole job.

      A line

      Bleeds includedN/A Check boxN/AFlag
      • To check a line, click anywhere on it. On a flagged line, click its check box. Check all, beside a section's title, checks every line in it that nobody has answered yet. Undo takes back exactly those.
      • N/A: the line does not apply. A line marked N/A does not count against a pass.
      • The flag button: something is wrong. Say what in the box that opens. A flag stops the block from passing.

      The right side

      Each block has its own buttons, such as Mark passed. The right side holds the rest, such as Send for review and Send to CSR. Its Activity list shows what was done on the job's QC and by whom, newest first. Every check is recorded too, but not listed.

      Everything saves as you go. If you close the window with something not sent, it asks first. Press Cancel to go back and send it.

      The CSR's part

      Handing a new job to a department

      On the work order's Prepress tab, press Send to Prepress. On the Tech Services tab it is Send to Tech Services. Type what they need to know and press Send.

      • Your line goes in the email and the QC activity. The email to the department opens in Outlook for you to send. The job list then reads NEW JOB.
      • Pressed it by mistake? Press it again, then Sent by mistake. A second email opens, telling them to ignore the first.
      • Only CSRs see these buttons, and only until the department picks the job up.
      • Pressing it is optional. A department can start QC Review on any job without it.

      The CSR checklist

      On the CSR tab, check the finished sign-off proofs after the departments. Only CSRs fill it in. It has no Corrections needed. When something is wrong, sign the job back in.

      Signing a job back in

      When the client asks for changes, or anything else needs another round, sign the job back in. Only a CSR can.

      1. Update the work order instructions and attach the new PDF in Midnight outside the app.
      2. To flag lines on your CSR checklist after passing it, press Reopen first.

      Covering a review

      Any CSR can check a department's checklist once it is sent for review. It is for when nobody in the department can. Your checks count. You cannot press Send for review, Fixed or Send to CSR.

      The operator's part

      The operator is whoever pressed Send for review that round, usually whoever did the work. Every round needs it. Until someone presses it, the department's checklist is read only for everyone.

      • Send for review: anyone on that department's list may press it. Add a note for the reviewer if you like, then press Send it. An email to the rest of the department opens for you to send.
      • Once you have sent it, you cannot review it. Someone else checks it.
      • When a reviewer sends a piece back, they email you. Fix what is flagged, on the work order or in the files. Then press Fixed, ready for re-review. It goes back to whoever sent it back, and an email to them opens.
      • A piece the CSR signed back in while your round was going comes straight to you, marked Signed back in by the CSR. Do the change, then press Fixed. Until you do, nobody can check it.
      • When every block has passed, the reviewer emails you "QC passed". Press Send to CSR, paste the paths and press Send it. An email to the CSR opens with the paths. Anyone on the department's list may send them.
      • If a block is reopened after the files went, the window says so. On the Prepress tab, so does an added piece. Once every block has passed again, press Send again.
      • Someone else on the department's list can ask you to send the files. The window then says who asked.

      The reviewer's part

      Anyone on that department's list except the operator may check the work. A CSR may cover. Whoever changes something first is shown as the Reviewer.

      • Open each block and go through the lines: check, N/A or flag.
      • Mark passed works once every line is answered and nothing is flagged. A passed block is locked. To change it, press Reopen.
      • Corrections needed, then Send it back, sends the block to the operator with your flags. Once a block is back with the operator, Corrections needed stays grey until you add a flag or change a note.
      • When the operator presses Fixed, they email you. Check off each flagged line that is fixed. Then pass the block, or send it back again.
      • A CSR change that joined your round says, after Fixed, Check every line again: it came back from the CSR. Otherwise the block comes back cleared, and the tab says what came back.
      • Ask the operator to send them shows once every block has passed and the files have not gone. Anyone on the department's list except the operator can press it. An email to the operator opens. Your own Send to CSR goes grey while your ask stands.

      Signed sheets

      Every pass freezes the sheet as it was signed. It keeps:

      • each check, N/A and N/A reason, and each flag
      • who signed it and when
      • that department's instructions as they stood (both departments on the CSR's sheet)

      To see one, press See the signed sheet on a passed entry in the Activity list. An entry with several blocks passed together has a link for each, named after the block.

      Export prints it or saves it as a PDF. So does Ctrl+P while the sheet is open.

      Export all, on the right of the Activity heading, prints every signed sheet of the job in one PDF, oldest first.

      The emails

      The app never sends an email. When your press would tell somebody, a new Outlook message opens on your screen, filled in. You press Send.

      • To, the subject and the words are filled in. You can add a name or a line first.
      • The email comes from your own mailbox, like one you wrote yourself.
      • The record is in QC Review. The activity says "Email opened in Outlook for" and the names in To.
      • You are never put in To for your own press.
      • Someone whose name is not linked to a user is left out. The job's activity says so.
      • Someone whose Email switch is off in Manage CSRs & Assignees is left out too. When the email was for that one person, the job's activity names them.
      • A department with a group address gets four emails there, not each name: new job, Sent by mistake, Send for review, sign back in.
      • Each email has an Open QC Review link. It opens the window on that job, tab and piece.
      • The new job and signed back in emails also have Open work order 48127. It opens the work order for editing, like clicking its row.
      • A press that tells two groups opens two messages, one after the other.

      Archiving, deleting or importing a job with QC

      • Every way to archive first warns you, unless the job is at CSR PASSED. The warning says where QC Review has got to, and Archiving takes it off the QC list and locks its reviewers out until it is restored. It never stops you.
      • To read an archived job's QC, open the Archived list, press View on the job, then QC Review. It is read only.
      • Deleting a job keeps its QC record. It opens from Deleted jobs at the bottom of the job list.
      • Import warns when the file leaves checklists without their piece.

      Deleted jobs

      Deleted jobs, under the Archived list, lists every deleted job that had QC Review, newest first. Its QC record button opens the job's QC history and signed sheets, read only.

      Words

      Operator
      Whoever pressed Send for review this round, usually whoever did the work. Cannot check their own work.
      Reviewer
      Whoever checks the work: anyone else on that department's list, or a CSR covering.
      Round
      One trip through QC Review. A sign back in starts the next one. If a department's round is still going, the sign back in joins it.
      Sign back in
      The CSR sends a job back to one or both departments for another round.
      N/A
      The line does not apply to this job. It does not count against a pass.
      Flag
      The line is wrong, with a note saying what needs fixing. A flagged block cannot pass.
      Piece
      A component on the work order (the Letter, the Envelope).
